**FILM/CINEMA/MUSIC/PODCAST:** 03.1–03.8. Actual workflow pitch → script/score → storyboard/shot list → approved shots/rigs/voices → production → continuity QA → editable timeline → export, with versioned job state, licenses, rollback and no fake rendered film. Podcast script in 02.6 and real recording/edit/voice cast in 03.5.

**GAME WORLDS:** Release 1 explicitly requires **one playable game**, real authored NPC choices, evidence-backed quests, authoritative state, save→quit→relaunch→reload test. Existing ECHO-7 prototype does not prove final Godot release. Dialogic (Godot add-on) first; YarnSpinner-Godot-GDScript is early-access and needs Godot 4.6+, so not an unreviewed R1 requirement. Roblox Studio, Minecraft, UEFN, VRChat, ZEPETO and indie-game worlds are optional distinct external channels subject to platform rules.

**MODEL LAB:** official download after accepted user consent and pinned immutable revision, model/voice rights, true bytes/SHA, VRAM check, STOP/resume, LoRA only on authorized data with holdout and rollback. Reuse existing FORGE/EVOLUTION, not another trainer/inference backend.

**BRAND & REVENUE:** 05.1–05.7, distinct brands, channel factory, controlled publisher, sponsor CRM, revenues real vs forecast, growth metrics and IP licensing. No publication, account opening, paid ads or fake sales without verified credentials and explicit approval.

**NO-LOSS:** approved HOME/MENU/WORK and original five Persona identity assets byte-for-byte SHA; existing OneHost WebView2/NSIS/Ollama/RAG/Voice/STOP/ACL/events.v3, SourceLock 729/3. Do not merge commercial/AGPL/GPL code directly without license analysis. No new backend or independent fake CoreID.

3. **Story-to-Scene Compiler**: turn a verified scene outline into a shotlist, timeline, editable Fountain draft and an FFmpeg/Blender/ComfyUI plan without claiming generated footage.
