6. Periodically perform existing full-parameter teacher-to-student distillation/continued training on mixed verified data and replay data to check forgetting. LoRA can itself be used for distillation: distillation describes the teacher supervision, not whether all parameters are trainable. Transitioning LoRA to full-parameter training creates a new optimizer configuration; do not pretend the adapter optimizer is a full-weight resume.
7. Quantize a validated merged candidate for existing serving engine only after benchmark (e.g. Q8/Q6/Q5 candidates according to actual engine support and memory). Keep an FP16/master reference.
